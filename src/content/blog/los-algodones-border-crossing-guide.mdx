Cross Los Algodones through the Andrade port. Start with its [latest lane reports and posted hours](/crossing/andrade/); confirm the operating window before making appointments or planning your return. A historical low wait does not guarantee today’s crossing.

## Read the published history

The chart uses separately published history. Check its source dates and sample coverage, then return to the current lane reports before leaving.

## Documents and goods

Use [INM’s official entry instructions](https://www.inm.gob.mx/fmme/publico/en/solicitud.html) for Mexico and [CBP’s guidance](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/clearing-customs/prohibited-restricted-items) for return requirements and restricted goods. Do not assume a short visit eliminates immigration paperwork. Eligibility depends on the traveler and journey.

For exemptions, declarations or medication, consult the [official customs guidance](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/know-before-you-visit/customs-duty-information) and CBP before buying or traveling. This guide does not give a universal allowance or medication quantity.

## Bringing a dog

Follow [CDC’s navigator](https://www.cdc.gov/importation/dogs/index.html) for the dog’s six-month travel history. Read our [corrected dog-entry guide](/blog/cdc-dog-import-rule-2024-mexico-border/) with links to the relevant CDC pathways.

## Parking and walking

Confirm a parking operator’s exact address, hours, current price and walking route to the port.

This independent guide does not endorse a clinic, pharmacy or parking operator. [Check current reports](/crossing/andrade/) and [the methodology](/methodology/) before relying on a wait figure.
