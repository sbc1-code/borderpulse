**[Renew SENTRI in the official TTP portal (opens a new tab)](https://ttp.dhs.gov/)**

Start with your membership's expiration date on the TTP dashboard. This guide explains the renewal steps; Border Pulse does not accept applications or payments.

<OfficialSource agency="DHS Trusted Traveler Programs" url="https://ttp.dhs.gov/faq">
  Renewal opens one year before membership expiration. The official renewal FAQ describes continued benefits for renewals submitted before expiration. Check your TTP account and CBP instructions for your individual status.
</OfficialSource>

## Choose your next step

- **Ready to renew:** open the official portal above and select **Renew** in your SENTRI membership section.
- **Already submitted:** check your TTP dashboard for the application status and any requested action. For questions about continued benefits, consult the renewal FAQ and CBP support; this guide cannot confirm your lane access.
- **Expired without submitting:** the FAQ says renewal is still possible after expiration. Its statement about continued benefits applies to applications submitted before expiration. Check your account and CBP's instructions before traveling; do not assume that submitting late restores lane access.

## 1. Sign in and find your membership

Use [ttp.dhs.gov](https://ttp.dhs.gov/), the official application site. If you do not have a login.gov account, follow the portal's account setup instructions. The renewal FAQ explains how to associate your TTP profile with your **PASSID**, found on your Trusted Traveler card or approval letter.

On the dashboard, find your existing SENTRI membership and choose **Renew**. If the option is missing, first check the expiration date and whether you are within the one-year renewal window. Use the [official TTP FAQ](https://ttp.dhs.gov/faq) and its CBP support link for account-specific problems. Do not send login details, identity documents or payment information to Border Pulse.

## 2. Review the application and submit payment

Follow the renewal form's instructions and update the information it requests. Review each section before submitting; this guide does not replace the form's requirements for addresses, travel, employment or vehicles.

<OfficialSource agency="DHS Trusted Traveler Programs" url="https://ttp.dhs.gov/faq">
  The renewal instructions take applicants through Final Review, certification and the Purchase Summary. Check the total shown by TTP before paying. Application-processing fees are non-refundable. After payment, the portal returns you to the dashboard.
</OfficialSource>

Use the dashboard to check the submitted application. Opening or saving a form is not the same as completing submission and payment. This guide does not quote a fee or promise a processing deadline; use the amount and instructions shown in your account.

## 3. Check status and interview instructions

<OfficialSource agency="DHS Trusted Traveler Programs" url="https://ttp.dhs.gov/faq">
  An interview may not be required for renewal. TTP instructs members to check the site periodically for the actions needed to finish their renewal.
</OfficialSource>

Follow the instructions attached to your application, including any interview request. **Conditional approval by itself is not a statement that you may use a SENTRI lane.** Do not confuse an application's review stage with your membership's benefits. For a renewal submitted before expiration, start with the FAQ's continued-benefits rule above and check your account for any other restriction or action.

If your status, membership or travel eligibility is unclear, use CBP support through the [official FAQ](https://ttp.dhs.gov/faq). Border Pulse cannot confirm individual eligibility or resolve an application.

## 4. Activate your SENTRI card

<OfficialSource agency="DHS Trusted Traveler Programs" url="https://ttp.dhs.gov/faq">
  The card-activation FAQ says SENTRI members should always activate their cards. Follow the activation instructions in your TTP account when your card arrives.
</OfficialSource>
