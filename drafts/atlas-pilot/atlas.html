About this coverage

How to read these reports

This file stores a single capture. A reported wait is an agency estimate. Canada’s figure excludes traffic on approach roads. U.S. lane reports measure the queue to the first inspection booth. Each direction has its own source and report clock.

For this preview, a U.S. lane report older than two hours at capture appears as unknown, with the earlier estimate below it. This two-hour limit is a test setting. Land colors and map pins show geography and selection. A whole-border condition rating requires broader coverage.
